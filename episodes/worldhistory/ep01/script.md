# World History on the Map · Ep 1: Napoleon invades Russia, 1812

Format: 9:16 vertical long-form, one continuous video, news look (same as the Thai history channel).
Question for the whole video: **"Where did Europe's greatest army go?"**
Voice: one English narrator, Gemini TTS, Sadaltager. 80 beats, ~1,470 words ≈ 10 min with pauses (68 approved first, 12 added 21 Oct to reach 10 min).
VO text below is the on-screen caption; `beats.json` holds the spoken form (numbers as words, via `../speak.mjs`).

Signature visual: **Minard's army band** (width = men, 1 mm = 10,000 on his map), tan going in, black coming
back, with a live troop counter on its head. It runs through the whole video.
