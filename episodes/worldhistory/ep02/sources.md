# Sources · Episode 2: Hannibal crosses the Alps, 218 BC

Checked online 22 Oct 2026. Ancient numbers are the ancient authors' figures (Polybius, Livy); modern historians
often think the big ones are inflated, and the script says "Polybius says" where that matters. Britannica (403)
and Perseus could not be fetched; the primary texts were read on LacusCurtius and archive.org.
