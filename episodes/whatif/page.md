## Description (YouTube; Facebook can use the first two paragraphs + hashtags)

In 1867, Russia sold Alaska to the United States for just $7.2 million, about two cents an acre, for more land than Texas, California and Montana combined. Why did the Tsar sell? And if he hadn't, would Alaska be Canadian today, or Soviet, with missiles on North American soil?

We follow the real story on satellite maps, then tear up the treaty and follow two what-if paths: the most likely one and the scariest one. Everything marked WHAT IF is speculation; everything else is sourced below.

If you were the Tsar in 1867, would you have sold? Tell us in the comments. 👇
